Discord Social SDK in Astra Mobile

SDK-enabled Android builds include Discord Social SDK 1.10.19337, provided by
Discord Inc. under the Discord Social SDK Terms. It is proprietary software and
is not licensed under Astra Mobile's GNU GPL license. Astra Mobile's separate,
Discord-only GPLv3 section 7 permission is in LICENSE-DISCORD-EXCEPTION.

The SDK is used only for optional Rich Presence through the installed Discord
Android app. The integration is off by default and requires a signed-in Discord
account and Discord activity sharing. Astra does not request Discord account
credentials, contact lists, messages, voice access, or an OAuth account link.

Discord Rich Presence is available only to people at least 13 years old who
meet Discord's minimum age requirement in their country. Use of Discord and
this integration is subject to Discord's applicable terms.

The SDK's unmodified open-source dependency notices are packaged as
assets/notices/discord/License-Notices.txt and can be read in Settings > Info >
Licenses. Those notices describe third-party components; they are not a license
for the proprietary Discord SDK itself. Astra is not endorsed by Discord.

SDK-free builds contain no Discord SDK binary or SDK dependency notices.
Obtain the SDK through Discord's Developer Portal if you wish to build a version
that includes it. The raw SDK, headers, and AAR must not be published in Astra's
source repository or as standalone release or CI artifacts.
